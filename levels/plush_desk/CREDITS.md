# The Plush Desk – credits

- Level, code, shaders, models, textures, music and sound effects: Rusty & Claude.
  - The stuffed shapes (keycaps, keyboard case, planets, mouse, mouse pad) and cardboard
    Claude are made in Blender by script: `source/make_models.py` inflates sewn panels with
    a cloth pressure simulation, `source/make_cardboard.py` builds Claude from boxes,
    tubes, tape and matchboxes. Run them with Blender's Python module (`pip install bpy`).
  - The fabric and cardboard textures are generated by `source/make_textures.py`.
  - The music and the sounds are composed in code (`source/make_audio.py`, with the
    project's tiny synth).
- **Fredoka** (`fonts/`) – © The Fredoka Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (see `fonts/OFL.txt`). Used for the embroidered key legends and the planet tags.

Inspired by the craft worlds of games where players build levels together. Nothing is
taken from any of them: no characters, names, sounds, music, menus or assets.
