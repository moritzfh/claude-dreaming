# The Plush Desk – credits

- Level, code, shaders, models, textures, music and sound effects: Rusty & Claude.
  - The stuffed shapes (keycaps, keyboard case, planets, mouse, mouse pad, felt blocks) and
    cardboard Claude are made in Blender by script: `source/make_models.py` inflates sewn
    panels with a cloth pressure simulation, `source/make_cardboard.py` builds Claude from
    boxes, tubes, tape and matchboxes. Run them with Blender's Python module (`pip install bpy`).
  - The fabric and cardboard textures are generated by `source/make_textures.py`.
  - The music and the sounds are composed in code with the project's tiny synth:
    `source/make_audio.py` (the desk's lullaby and all the sound effects) and
    `source/make_story_music.py` (the theme of "First Stitches", the sewing-machine chase,
    the yarn tower, the theatre waltz, the quilt and the curtain fanfare). Every melody is
    written note by note in those files.
  - `source/story_bot.gd` is a test pilot that plays "First Stitches" from the first page to
    the zipper pockets (`--pd_story --pd_bot`).
- **Fredoka** (`fonts/`) – © The Fredoka Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (see `fonts/OFL.txt`). Used for the embroidered key legends, the planet tags and the signs.

## Thank you, LittleBigPlanet

This dream is an open love letter to **LittleBigPlanet** (Media Molecule). Three friends on
one couch, levels drawn on paper and built together, the first logic circuits made of
cardboard – that is where the wish to make worlds like this one began. The STORY world ends
in a little theatre with a thank-you quilt that says so.

Everything here is made from scratch: no characters, sounds, music, menus, fonts or assets
from LittleBigPlanet are used, and no music from it is quoted. The name appears only in
this note and on the thank-you quilt.

An unofficial fan homage. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Interactive Entertainment
or Media Molecule.
