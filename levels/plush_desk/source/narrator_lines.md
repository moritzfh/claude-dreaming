# First Stitches – the narrator's lines

One file per line in `levels/plush_desk/audio/narrator/`, named after the key (`<key>.ogg`, `.wav` or `.mp3`). Mono is fine, 44.1 or 48 kHz, a little silence (about 0.1 s) at the start and end, no music or effects underneath. Lines marked *(name)* show the player's name in the subtitle: record them without it.

The voice: a warm, gentle storyteller reading a bedtime story to a child, a little dry humour, never shouting. Not an imitation of a real person.
