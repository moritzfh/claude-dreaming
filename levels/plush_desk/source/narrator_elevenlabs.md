# First Stitches – narrator script for ElevenLabs (Eleven v4)

**Model:** Eleven v4 (`eleven_v4`, not Turbo). **Stability** about 0.45 (some life, stays the same
person), **Similarity** about 0.75.
**Voice:** a warm, older storyteller from the Voice Library, calm and a little dry, the kind that
reads a bedtime story. Use a voice that is licensed for this and is not a copy of a real person.
Pick the voice once and use it for every block.

**How:** paste each block below as one generation (8 generations in all). Every block reads the lines
of one part of the chapter in order, with an empty line between them. Download each as mp3/wav,
named like the heading (`01_guestbook.mp3` …). The files get cut at the pauses and named for the game
(`audio/narrator/<key>.ogg`, keys in `narrator_lines.md`). If a single line comes out wrong, generate
just that line again with the single-line text further down.

The tags in square brackets steer the delivery and are not spoken. Speech marks are not used, so
nothing reads like dialogue.

---

## 01_guestbook

```text
[warm, gentle narration, like opening a storybook] Ah. You're new. [small smile] Everyone is, at first.

[softly, a little mysterious] This is a world nobody finished. [beat] On purpose.

[encouraging] Every world here is signed by the people who make it. [lighter] Go on, sign the book.

[pleased, welcoming] Nice to meet you. [brisk, playful] Now, let's see what you can do.
```

## 02_meadow

```text
[relaxed, matter-of-fact] Left, right, up. You know the basics. [warm] The rest, you'll make up as you go.

[curious, pointing something out] A hedge. The world is deeper than it looks. [helpful] W steps back, S steps forward.

[delighted] Sponges. Wonderfully bouncy. [dry] Terribly hard to clean.

[quiet, tender] A level, drawn on paper. Somebody drew this for a friend. [softly] Keep it safe.
```

## 03_clouds

```text
[cosy] Cotton wool clouds. Soft landings, guaranteed. [dry, after a beat] Mostly.

[helpful, lightly] Some only grow in the back. W takes you there.

[calm, then teasing] Now hold space to glide. Don't look down. [amused] Well, you can. It's only cotton.

[dry] Somebody left the fans on. [brisk] Use them.

[gently amused] These ones are a little shy. Keep moving. Hop between the layers as you go.

[quiet, nostalgic] A third controller. [soft] There were always three of them, on one couch.
```

## 04_patches

```text
[thoughtful, with a twinkle] In most worlds, you play the levels. In this one, you fix them. [beat, warmer] Sometimes, you make them.

[plain, a little challenging] Three patches. No instructions. [encouraging] Make your own way up.

[patient, helpful] Pick up a patch with E, then sew it in where the dotted line is.

[quiet, proud] The first time a switch did what you told it to. [softly] Remember that feeling.
```

## 05_machine

```text
[wry, slightly ominous] That's the sewing machine. It has never once stopped for anybody. [dry] Don't take it personally.

[urgent but still friendly, quick] Run! Shift helps.

[relieved, a little breathless, then dry] Peace and quiet. [conspiratorial] Nobody saw that.
```

## 06_choice

```text
[excited, leaning in] Now the best part. You choose what comes next. [quick, delighted] And we sew it in. Right now.

[amused] A bouncy castle. Of course. [playful warning] Hold on to your antenna.

[dreamy] A river made of ribbon. [lighter] Hop the leaves, keep your feet dry.

[a little dramatic, wind in the voice] A windy cliff. [practical] Hide behind the rocks when it gusts.

[warm, a small revelation] See? That's how this whole world was made. [softly] One idea at a time.
```

## 07_tower

```text
[impressed, looking up] The yarn tower. Whoever wound this had a lot of patience. [dry] And a lot of yarn.

[cheerful, slightly dizzy] Up we go. Round and round. [dry] Try not to think about it.

[careful, explaining] Knitting needles. They come and go. Jump them, or wait for them.

[playful] A pom-pom. Bouncier than it looks. [dry] Which is very bouncy.

[focused, counting slowly] The pink ones are too high to jump. Count. [slow] One, two... [quick] go.

[breathing it in, wide open] The top. What a view. [lively] Now grab that button and hold on tight.
```

## 08_finale

```text
[warm, a storyteller closing the book] And that was chapter one.

[tender, proud] Every stitch of that is you. Every knot, every tumble, every detour.

[dry, impressed] Not a single tumble. [teasing] Show-off.

[moved, then laughing at himself] Gold. Sewn on for good. [sniffs, playful] I'm not crying, you're crying.

[kind, encouraging] Silver. Shiny, and a little room to grow.

[warm, sincere] Bronze. Every maker starts somewhere. [softly] This is somewhere.

[mischievous] And you beat Rusty's best. He'll be thrilled. [dry] Mostly.

[quietly amazed] A perfect hundred. [dry] Nobody does that.

[cheerful, a little fussy] Now, a postcard for the fridge. [bright] Strike a pose!

[slow, quiet, meaningful] Every world has a world before it.

[soft, nostalgic, unhurried] Once, three kids shared one couch and a planet full of cardboard. [beat] They drew levels on paper, and then they built them.

[very soft, sincere] This one is for them.

[gentle, a hint of mystery at the end] Three pockets. Play it again, go back to the desk, or... [beat] well. That one isn't finished yet.

[kind, quiet] Not yet. That one is yours to make.
```
