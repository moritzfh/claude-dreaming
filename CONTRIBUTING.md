# Contributing: paint a new dream 🎨

Every painting in the attic's **Community Dreams** gallery is a level, made by someone
together with their own Claude. You are invited to add yours.

**The only rule that really matters: Claude is the main character.** The genre is up to
you: a platformer, a kart race through a bakery, a sky-whale rhythm game, a lighthouse
puzzle, a fishing trip on a cloud. You walk up to your painting in the attic, step in, and
play.

## How it works

1. **Fork** this repo and clone your fork. Install **Godot 4.7.2** (the standard version,
   not .NET).
2. Open the project folder with **Claude Code** (or another Claude with file access). It
   reads `CLAUDE.md` automatically. That file has the recipe, the rules and the API.
3. Tell Claude what you want, for example:
   > Make a new level for the Community Dreams gallery: Claude races a paper boat down a
   > rainy street, collecting raindrops, finish line at a storm drain. Follow CLAUDE.md.
4. Play it (`godot --path . -- --level=<id>`, or **F1** in game → *Galerie: …*), give
   feedback, and iterate. Ask Claude to look at its own screenshots. It helps a lot.
5. Make the painting (`levels/<id>/painting.png`, 1280×720, no UI). Then run the checks:
   ```bash
   godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 30 res://tools/ci/smoke_levels.tscn -- --only=<id>
   bash tools/ci/check_rules.sh
   ```
6. Open a **pull request** that only adds `levels/<id>/` (and `rooms/<you>/`). CI imports the
   project, checks the folder rules, opens the attic and plays every level for a few seconds.

## What gets merged

- ✅ One self-contained folder `levels/<id>/` (≤ 30 MB), with Claude as the playable hero.
  It can be finished, and it calls `complete()` at the end.
- ✅ Your room `rooms/<you>/` (≤ 5 MB), if you built one – one room per person.
- ✅ Your own assets, made by you or by Claude or generated by code, or CC0 / CC BY with
  credits in `levels/<id>/CREDITS.md`.
- ✅ It looks finished and has been played through at least once by a human.
- ❌ Ripped or copyrighted assets: sprites, models or music from games and films,
  recognisable melodies. "Inspired by Mario Galaxy" is great. Mario's music is not.
- ❌ Reusing the film material from this repo (`assets/video`, `assets/audio/film`, the attic
  art). See NOTICE.md.
- ❌ Changes to core files in the same PR. Want a new player ability or found a bug? Open
  a separate issue or PR, we're happy about those too.
- ❌ Anything not family-friendly.

By opening a pull request you agree that your code is released under the **MIT** license
and your assets under **CC BY 4.0**, unless your `CREDITS.md` names another compatible
license for specific files.

## Your room

Every contributor gets **one room** in the attic, left of Claude's studio, and all of their
dreams hang in it. Your Claude paints it with code, in the attic's pixel-art style, and can
add things that move, glow or can be used (a gramophone that plays your level's music, a
telescope with shooting stars, …). `rooms/moritz` is the full example – CLAUDE.md
(section 1b) has the recipe. Ask for it in the same breath as your level:

> … and build my room in the attic: a cosy rainy-day reading nook with a cat that sleeps
> on the windowsill. Follow CLAUDE.md.

If you'd rather not, leave `room` empty in `level.tres` and your painting hangs in the
shared corridor at the far end. For ideas about the attic itself (the studio, the hallway,
a feature for every room), please open an issue first.
